About

Engineer at the intersection of design and code

For over sixteen years I've worked across the full cycle of web development — from early e-commerce and CMS builds to leading front-end architecture at Visa, where I spent 13+ years shipping accessible, high-traffic digital experiences, including the rebuild of Visa.com.

As a Lead Design Engineer, I bridge design and engineering: translating complex designs into responsive, scalable, ADA-compliant interfaces, mentoring developers, and defining front-end best practices inside large design systems.

Most recently I've been working on AI-powered design engineering — LLM-based design review, RAG knowledge platforms, and computer-vision analysis of UI — bringing machine intelligence into the design workflow itself.

  • Accessibility first: WCAG 2.1 / ADA-compliant interfaces as a default, not an afterthought
  • Design systems: scalable, visually consistent component architecture
  • AI + UI: LLM judges, RAG retrieval, and computer vision for design intelligence
  • Mentorship: growing front-end developers and setting engineering standards
