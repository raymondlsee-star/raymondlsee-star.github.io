How to play
  • The board is a 3×3 grid of small tic-tac-toe boards. Win a small board by getting three in a row inside it.
  • The square you play in decides which small board your opponent must play in next — play the top-right square, and they play in the top-right board.
  • If that board is already won or full, your opponent gets a free move and can play anywhere.
  • Win three small boards in a row — across, down, or diagonally — to win the game.
  • The board you must play in glows gold. X always goes first.
